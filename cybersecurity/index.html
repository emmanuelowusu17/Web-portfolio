Protect Your Accounts. Stay Safe Online.

Cybercriminals look for easy targets. A few simple habits make you much harder to scam or hack. This guide shows you how to spot phishing, create strong passwords, and turn on multifactor authentication (MFA).

Why It Matters

Phishing messages and weak passwords are two of the most common ways criminals break into accounts. Once they get in, they can:

Steal your identity

Use your personal information to pretend to be you.

Take your money

Get into bank, payment, or shopping accounts.

Take over your accounts

Lock you out of your email, school, or social media.

3 Things You Can Do Today

  1. Before you click a link in an email or text, stop and check who really sent it.
  2. Change any password you use on more than one account.
  3. Turn on MFA for your email account first, because it can be used to reset all your other passwords.
