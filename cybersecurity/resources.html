Where to Report

Reporting helps protect other people from the same scam.
If you got... Report it here
A phishing email Forward it to reportphishing@apwg.org
A phishing text message Forward it to SPAM (7726)
Any scam or fraud ReportFraud.ftc.gov (Federal Trade Commission)
Your personal information stolen IdentityTheft.gov (step-by-step recovery plan)
An internet crime that cost you money IC3.gov (FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center)

Sources

Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA): Secure Our World

Simple steps for staying safe online: recognize phishing, use strong passwords, turn on MFA, and update software. Used on every page of this site.

https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world

Federal Trade Commission (FTC): How to Recognize and Avoid Phishing Scams

Warning signs of phishing emails and texts, and what to do if you get one. Used on the Phishing page and for where to report.

https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-and-avoid-phishing-scams

National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST): Digital Identity Guidelines

Official U.S. guidance on passwords and multifactor authentication. Used for the password myths and the MFA method ranking.

https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/

FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3): 2025 Internet Crime Report

Data on internet crime complaints and losses reported to the FBI. Used for the phishing loss statistic on the Home page.

https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf

Information based on guidance from CISA, the FTC, NIST, and the FBI.
